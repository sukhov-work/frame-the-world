## What shipped (mechanics for future maintenance)

### Day arcs (lib/ephemeris/dayArc.ts pure + globe/scene/dayArcs.ts renderer)
- `sampleDayArc(body, sceneMs, lat, lon, {stepMin 10, tickEveryH 1})` → 145 pts + 25 hour ticks
  across the LOCAL SOLAR day (`solarOffsetHours = round(lon/15)` — the captureTime convention;
  **NOT store/time.localDateStr, that's browser-TZ**). Points carry `utcMs` + `t01` (0..1 day
  fraction). `dayFraction(arc, ms)` = the shader's uNow01. `azAltToEnu` (E,N,U) tested vs bearings.
- Renderer: ONE group anchored at `camera.position`, scale = the SAME impostor distance clamp as
  sky.ts (`clamp(far·SKY.impostorFarFrac, near·1.2, far·0.95)`) → **the sun/moon discs sit ON
  their arcs by construction** (verified live + at the 2026-07-29 full moon). Geometry = unit
  ENU→ECEF dirs on the anchor's `enuBasis`; attrs `aT01` (past/future via `step(uNow01, vT)` —
  scrubbing NEVER rebuilds) + `aFade` (horizon melt smoothstep over altDeg DAYARC.horizonFade
  −6°→−1°). Rebuild only when anchor moved >1e-7° or sceneMs left [startMs, endMs) (day-cross
  verified — midnight walk kept the arc). Anchor = photo `upload.placement` | `camera.tempPin`.
- **TRAP (browser-bisected): additive lines are INVISIBLE against the bright day sky** (added
  ~10-30/255; what looked like arcs were pin stems). Arc materials are ALPHA-blended solid
  strokes (PhotoPills grammar: sun tokens.sunGlow / moon tokens.moonlight), depthTest FALSE
  (analytic horizon owns occlusion — the fake camera-anchored distance makes the depth buffer
  lie), **renderOrder 10 per-OBJECT (Group.renderOrder does NOT propagate to children)**.
- Tunables: `DAYARC` group (alphas past 0.22/future 0.6, tickAlphaGain 1.25, tickHalfDeg 0.45,
  fadeTauMs 250).
