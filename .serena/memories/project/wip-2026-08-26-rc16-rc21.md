## RC16 — the audit's fix was refuted by measuring the thing it was named after
The charter/audit sized RC16 as a WIDENED Overpass harvest + a margin bake + an inverse-prism
crossfade ring: a 62 MB re-fetch, all 16 OSM2World converts re-run (~41 min for Dnipro), a new
`marginM` key, and a `regions.ts` bbox change it is machine-pinned against. **None of that was
needed, and the premise was wrong.**

New read-only `scripts/bake/measure-straddlers.mjs` (writes nothing, downloads nothing uncached;
companion to `terrain/measure-dsm-signature.mjs`) re-runs both bakers' *inputs* to the point where
each picks a grid cell and classifies the decision into four buckets — IN / C_IN (centroid inside,
crosses an edge) / C_OUT (centroid outside, still touches) / FAR (disjoint).

**`droppedOutside` was TWO POPULATIONS WEARING ONE NAME.** 96–99.9 % of it is FAR — a **median
761 m / 40 km / 36 km** outside the bbox, max **55 km / 653 km / 149 km** — because OSM2World
renders its whole relation-recursed extract, whose data bounds inflate far past the sub-box (the
adapter's own comment already said "with `>;` relation recursion the data bounds inflate to 100+
km"; nobody connected that to the drop counter). Dropping those is CORRECT. Only 123/61/1 were real
straddlers, and dropping THOSE is what left a half-building notch: the runtime prism
(`bboxClipPrismEcef`) had already erased the inside half of their Cesium twin.

**The margin bake is unnecessary because the straddlers were ALREADY HARVESTED.**
`way["building"](bbox)` returns whole ways that INTERSECT the bbox (`out geom` inlines their full
geometry), and OSM2World's extract is wider still. There was never a data gap — only a rule that
threw the data away. This is also why **extruder FAR = 0 at every city**: its unbounded clamp was
safe by a property of its INPUT, not of its code.

**THE RULE** (`scripts/bake/lib/geo.mjs`, `binFeature` + `bboxIntersects` / `bboxContainsBounds` /
`cellOf`, imported by both bakers — it was inline in each `main()` with zero test coverage):
- **OWNERSHIP by bbox INTERSECTION, never by centroid.** The contract is "the enriched tileset
  draws whatever the prism removed", so anything poking into the prism is ours or it is a hole.
- **PLACEMENT by the clamped centroid cell.** Ownership guarantees the feature touches the bbox, so
  the centroid is at most one feature-radius outside → the clamp moves it ≤1 cell. A clamp, not a
  teleport.

## RC21 — shipped OFF, heartbeat-first, and the charter's premise refused on evidence
`src/lib/globe/frameGate.ts` + `GATE` in tuning + `window.__frameGate` + 12 tests.
The charter asked for a predicate covering every animating source. That is refused on the 2026-08-26
recon's own numbers (40+ sources / 20 files / ~14 asymptotic eases with NO snap): no such predicate
is both cheap and complete, and a false negative here is a **frozen globe**, not RC19's stale inset.
So `pipCache`'s shape is reused wholesale — **the heartbeat is the safety net, the epsilons are only
an optimisation.**
- `maxStaleMs: 200` bounds every miss to a low frame rate rather than a freeze.
- **`restMs: 6_000` is the RC21-only addition** and it is SIZED, not chosen: the repo's own settling
  convention is ≥6.2τ, and the slowest LOOK-only ease is `ULTRA.exposureTauMs` 950 ms (haze 700,
  reveal 600, drape 350 under it) → 6.2 × 950 ≈ 5,890. Camera eases (`altEaseTauMs` 2,600,
  `poseEaseTauMs` 1,200) are excluded on purpose — they move the camera, so the pose predicate
  already holds the clock reset for their whole duration. **Machine-checked by a unit test**, which
  is how the first draft caught itself naming `SKY.exposureTauMs` (a field that does not exist →
  `NaN`).
- `markGateDirty()` covers tier applies, the bloom/flat flip, the ULTRA chip, resize, context
  restore. **Tile streaming and every StylizedTiles ease are covered by the heartbeat ALONE** —
  design, not gap, and the reason `maxStaleMs` may not be raised casually.
- **Both rollbacks unit-LOCKED**, not asserted in prose: `enabled: false` (the shipped default) and
  `maxStaleMs: 0` each return true unconditionally.
- **It buys GPU and power, NOT CPU** — everything above the gate, including `tilesHandle.update()`'s
  whole 55-step chain, still runs every frame. Say it that way.
- `composer.render()` and the PiP blit skip TOGETHER and cannot be separated: with
  `preserveDrawingBuffer: false` a blit into a skipped frame's backbuffer draws into undefined
  contents.

**The default flip is an owner call and is browser-UNVERIFIED** — it wants a full ULTRA-timelapse
soak via `__frameGate.enabled = true` (counters are CUMULATIVE — sample twice and difference).
