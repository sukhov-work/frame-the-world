- `pathAltitude(e, h0, h1, bump, floorM, rampFrac)`: blend+bump clamped from below by floorM,
  ramped over rampFrac (0.2) from each endpoint's own altitude → endpoints EXACT, mid-path ≥ floor.

  hops keep plain slerp (their pan IS the point). orientInFrac 0.15→0.3 browser-tuned (334→257°/s

- `arrivalPose({lookAt, approachHoriz, groundAltM, altAboveGroundM, tiltDeg, wgs84A/B})` — THE one
  arrival derivation: camAlt = max(ground+altAbove, hLook+1); back-off = drop·tan(tilt). Consumers:

  lookAt = plane centre, approach = −forward horizontal, north fallback) + flyRequest (SEARCH 52°,

- `flight.start(target, {floorM})` — opts param; FPV entry passes no floor (apex may sit below
  coarse floor).

### TRAPS (browser-found, load-bearing)
1. **`ground.heightAt` returns NEGATIVE garbage on unloaded/coarse quantized-mesh tiles** (Dnipro
   sampled from Kyiv → sank the arrival to its hLook+1 safety floor at 97 m). EVERY consumer clamps
   [0, 9000] — clamp-only-upward discipline.
2. **Underground camera unloads the ENTIRE ground tileset** (0 meshes — nothing passes SSE from
   under the surface) → live heightAt AND GlobeControls adjustHeight (down-ray from camera) can
   never recover. Prevention only: sticky `lastGroundM`.
3. **Tiles only exist inside the view frustum** — sampling terrain at the CAMERA FOOTPRINT stays
   null at oblique tilt (verified through a whole 6 km dive). Sample at the VIEW FOCUS (_focus).
4. **GlobeControls.update() early-returns entirely when disabled** — near/far fit included. FPV
   calls `controls.adjustCamera(camera)` manually each frame (with `controls.adjustHeight = false`
   or cameraRadius pushes the camera off the apex).
5. EXIF capturedAt solar-time seeding re-pins scene time at placement — expected (Phase 4 design),
   surprises time-pinned verification scripts.

  y=±1.0 = photographer's exact frame); exit = fovTarget POSE 38 + frameArrivalPose flight.
